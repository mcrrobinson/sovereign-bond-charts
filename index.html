Bubble size is how far the currency has moved since 2020, measured by the BIS nominal effective exchange rate against a trade-weighted basket of 64 other currencies. A solid bubble is stronger than its 2020 average; a hollow ring is weaker. The ten euro countries share one currency, so they all use the euro-area index and have the same bubble.

Bubble size is how far the currency moved against its trade-weighted basket that year; a solid bubble means it strengthened and a hollow ring means it weakened.
